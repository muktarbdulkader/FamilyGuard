# FamilyGuard — Video Generation Prompts & Commercial Showcase Script

This document contains production-ready prompts, scene-by-scene storyboards, and scripts designed for modern AI video generators (such as **Runway Gen-3, OpenAI Sora, Kling AI, Luma Dream Machine, Pika Labs, and Google Veo**), as well as human video creators.

---

## 🎬 1. Master AI Video Prompt (All-in-One Cinematic Commercial)

**Target Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (Landscape) or 9:16 (Vertical)  
**Best For:** Runway Gen-3 Alpha, Sora, Kling 1.5, Google Veo

```text
Cinematic commercial for a sleek modern mobile app called "FamilyGuard". Ultra high-definition, photorealistic 4K, 60fps, commercial product lighting. 

Scene begins with a split screen: on the left side, a teenager looking at a modern smartphone trying to open a social app, suddenly a clean, vibrant royal indigo and violet gradient overlay appears with a shield icon reading "Permission Required: Smart Guard Protected". The teen presses a glowing "Ask Parent" button.

Instantly on the right side, a smiling parent's smartphone screen lights up with a premium notification card showing "App Request: 30 Min Access". The parent smoothly taps a glowing green "Approve 30m" button. 

Camera zooms into the child's screen as the overlay dissolves with a smooth particle transition, automatically launching the app with a floating green countdown timer pill showing "30m granted". Smooth camera pan, volumetric lighting, hyper-realistic UI animations, premium Apple-style aesthetic, vibrant indigo and violet brand palette (#4F46E5 to #7C3AED).
```

## 📱 2. Scene-by-Scene Storyboard Prompts (Full 60-Second Walkthrough)

### **Scene 1: Introduction & Unified Brand Aesthetic (0:00 - 0:08)**
* **Visual AI Prompt:**
  ```text
  Close-up isometric 3D render of two modern smartphones side by side floating on a minimalist frosted glass pedestal, soft studio lighting. Left screen displays "FamilyGuard Child Companion" with a Royal Indigo gradient shield. Right screen displays "Parental Control Portal" showing live screen-time analytics and connected children status. Soft purple neon ambient glow, ultra-clean UI, 4K render, 60fps.
  ```
* **Voiceover:**  
  *"Meet FamilyGuard — the modern parental control companion designed for balance, safety, and instant harmony."*

### **Scene 2: Child Opens a Restricted App & Instant Shield Appears (0:08 - 0:18)**
* **Visual AI Prompt:**
  ```text
  First-person POV shot of a teenager holding a smartphone in a bright modern living room. The teenager taps on a social media app icon (TikTok / Facebook). Within milliseconds, a sleek full-screen protection shield smoothly slides up with a gradient background of indigo and violet (#4F46E5 to #7C3AED), displaying "Smart Guard Protected: Permission Required" with a prominent "Ask Parent" button.
  ```
* **Voiceover:**  
  *"When a blocked or time-restricted application is opened, Smart Guard steps in with an instant, friendly protection shield."*

### **Scene 3: One-Tap Permission Request (0:18 - 0:26)**
* **Visual AI Prompt:**
  ```text

A subtle haptic wave ripple animation radiates across the button, and the text smoothly transitions to a green status badge: "Request Sent! Waiting for parent...". Fast blue data beam animation flows out of the device into the cloud.
